Beat the Models

Your call --°
  • 1 city
  • call its high in °F
  • locks at its midnight
  • 5 models to beat

The high is the NWS daily climate report's for the city's airport, midnight to midnight local standard time. Scoring: 100 for an exact call, 20 less for each °F off; off by 5 °F or more is a bust.

The models are each one's latest run out when the call locks: GFS, the GEFS mean, ECMWF, ICON and GEM, from their own maximum temperatures (GEM: the warmest of its hourly ones) at the airport, adjusted from the model's ground to the airport's elevation. AIFS, AIGFS, AIGEFS, HGEFS and the ECMWF ensemble mean only output every 6 hours, which misses the afternoon peak, so they sit out. These are the models' own numbers, so they won't match the 6-hourly maps.
