Daily weather games

A new puzzle every morning about yesterday's weather, the same for everyone. A couple of minutes each, no account needed.

  • Wx Marks the Spot Five of yesterday's weather extremes across the Lower 48. Drop a pin where each one happened.
  • Modeled or Measured Two maps of the same moment: one measured, one a forecast made days earlier. Pick the measured one.
