Modeled or Measured

Two maps of the same moment, five times. One shows what was measured; the other is a forecast for that moment made days earlier. Pick the measured one.

    Point at either map to compare the two there.

      A new puzzle every morning, around 6 a.m. Eastern. Tap a round above to look at it again.

      Run to Run Wx. Measured: NOAA's URMA (station observations analysed onto a 2.5 km grid) and MRMS (radar rainfall corrected to rain gauges). Run to Run Wx averages them onto the model's 0.25° grid and smooths them lightly, to about the model's level of detail, so sharpness can't give them away. Forecasts: NOAA's GFS. Basemap: Natural Earth.
